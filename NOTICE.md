## Items that need a specialist before classroom use
- Speech-language pathologist or AAC specialist: isolated speech sounds for students with apraxia of speech, dysarthria or no functional speech.
- Teacher of students with visual impairments: size, contrast and tactile formats of the cards and stroke guides.
- Teacher of the deaf or audiologist: the recorded sounds, and a non-sound alternative for the "Su sonido" step.
- Spanish speaker: review of sentences, syllable words and audio.
- Not tested with screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack) or with students.
